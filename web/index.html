How to read this

  • Amounts are administrative approvals (sanctioned ceilings), not money spent. Actual expenditure is not published on this page.
  • Revised orders and corrigenda restate earlier approvals, so they are excluded from totals; where the title gives old and new amounts the net change is shown separately.
  • Sector and agency are inferred from order titles by keyword rules and may be wrong; "Not stated in title" means the title does not name an implementing agency. Corrections go in data/overrides.json.
  • The PDFs are scanned images, so per-work line items are not extracted; each order links to its source PDF.
