How to read this

  • Amounts are administrative approvals (sanctioned ceilings), not money spent. Actual expenditure is not published on the source page.
  • Revision orders and corrigenda restate earlier approvals, so they are excluded from totals; disclosed old→new changes are shown as net revisions.
  • Sector and agency are inferred from order titles by keyword rules and can be wrong. Corrections go in data/overrides.json.
